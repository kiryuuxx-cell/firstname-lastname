About me

Sourcing 30 to 50 candidates per day for startup hiring teams.

Over the past two years I built candidate pipelines for early-stage and growth-stage startups using LinkedIn Recruiter, Ashby, and Handshake. The companies I worked with needed to hire across multiple roles on short timelines, with hiring managers who had no time to review weak shortlists. Every candidate I send needs to be worth their time.

I take the role brief, map the candidate profile, build my search strings, and run them until I have a shortlist that fits. I track every stage in Ashby and stay in close contact with hiring managers through Slack. When a candidate moves forward, I follow through. When they do not, I adjust the search.

What separates me from most recruiters at this stage: I am a Licensed Mechanical Engineer. When a startup needs to fill technical roles, I read job descriptions at a different level. I know which requirements are non-negotiable and which are flexible, and I can evaluate a candidate's background without relying on the hiring manager to explain the technical details.
